## Multi-board concepts

* **Machi-Point** — the board with the most traffic per period; can push ads to all Machi-Boards in its Machi-System. Evaluated monthly.
* **Machi-System** — the circle formed by a 7.5-min-walk radius around a Machi-Point; recomputed once per year; at launch drawn around the first board.
* **Machi-Verse** — a radius bookable only through machi15.com; large clients can place on all boards of a Machi-Verse.
* Boards are children of a Machi-Point (receive pushed ads; cannot push themselves).

## Hardware business model (idea)

Sell a 55" TV (~229 € net) + 36 months of machi15.com connection to the promoter (e.g. 360 € net ≈ 10 €/month), replacing the venue's "gammeliges schwarzes Brett" (old-fashioned corkboard).
