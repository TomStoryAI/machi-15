# Positioning

## Concept

Machi15 (machi15.com) — "Machi" (Stadt) + 15 (Minuten) — is a digital "schwarzes Brett" (bulletin board) for public spaces, framed around the 15-minute-city concept. The name doubles as a marketing angle for public funding (climate/urbanism trend); the technology side of 15-minute-city features comes in later versions.

## Target users

Everyone within roughly 7.5 minutes walking distance of a display (high-density areas) or 7.5 minutes by bike (less dense areas).

## MVP scope

A digital notice board that works **standalone per display** — each board is self-sufficient, no platform/marketplace effects needed. Optimized for 55" Google-TV displays (later: projectors for shop windows via rear projection).

## Core loop

1. Poster scans the on-screen QR ("Starte hier Dein kostenloses Inserat!") → mobile submit page
2. Anonymous ad: text-only or with photo (toggle), two text fields, category, 1 or 2 weeks runtime, optional contact fields (phone / WhatsApp / email / Instagram / address)
3. Flow: Opening → Approval → Doing → Ending (ad waits in approval queue, goes live, expires)
4. Viewers comment on live ads ("Kommentieren") — also moderated
5. Poster later views comments / deletes their ad via a management link (anonymous → token-based)

## Usability bar (hard)

Posting must feel **no more complex than taking a paper note, writing on it, and pinning it up**. Anonymous usage must be experiential — no email address or account is ever requested.

## Legal

Impressum + Datenschutz pages required (German public installation). Risk of unlawful content is accepted for the MVP (low reach); AI-based moderation in later versions.
